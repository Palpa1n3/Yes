              Hands-free & drivingDriving mode and the assistant button
            
            

              

                Driving mode keeps listening while Alton is on screen (phone in a car mount): say

                sends only after you say “send it”.

Choose Alton as the “Digital assistant app”; then long-press the home button
                    (or swipe up from a bottom corner) to open it already listening
              

            

          

          

            
              WidgetsHome-screen clock, battery, weather, screen time
            
            

              

                Alton has five widgets: Alton Clock, Alton Battery,
                Alton Weather, Alton Screen time and Alton. Each one changes
                with its size: drag its edges smaller or bigger (e.g. the clock shows just the time
                when small, the weather adds the next four days when big). To swipe between them
                like your others: add one (long-press the home screen › Widgets › Alton), long-press
                it › Create stack, and add the others to it.
